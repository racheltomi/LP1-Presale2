Frequently Asked Questions

When does HGA reopen?

Booking opens January 18, 2027, and grading begins February 1, 2027.

What happens after I purchase?

From January 18, we email you an invite to book your grading date on the HGA Grading Calendar. Then send your cards any time before your date.

When can I submit?

Any time before your booked grading date. Your confirmation page tells you when.

Which cards qualify?

We grade all sports and all TCG. Presale passes cover standard size cards. Booklets, folds and decks are booked separately.

Can I transfer my purchase?

No. Presale purchases can't be transferred.
